About RamJanaki Hotel

RamJanaki Hotel is a symbol of Nepali hospitality, located in the holy city of Janakpur — the birthplace of Goddess Sita. Our hotel is named after the divine couple Lord Ram and Goddess Sita, reflecting our commitment to warmth, devotion, and service.

With elegantly designed rooms, authentic Mithila cuisine, and warm Nepali hospitality, we ensure that every guest feels at home. Whether you are a pilgrim or a traveler, RamJanaki Hotel is your perfect home away from home.
